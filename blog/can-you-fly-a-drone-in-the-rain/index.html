Can You Fly a Drone in the Rain? What's Actually Safe

It's the question every pilot asks the first time clouds roll in mid-shoot: can I keep flying? The short answer is almost always no — not because it's illegal, but because the vast majority of drones have zero water protection and rain attacks the parts that keep you in the air. Here's what rain actually does, why even a light drizzle is riskier than it looks, and how to plan so you're never caught out.
