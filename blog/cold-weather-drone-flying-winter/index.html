Cold Weather Drone Flying: Why Winter Eats Your Flight Time

Winter is quietly the best flying weather of the year — the air is denser, the light is beautiful, and the crowds are gone. It's also when pilots lose the most drones. Cold doesn't hurt your aircraft's ability to fly; it attacks the battery, and it does it fast enough to turn a routine flight into an emergency landing. Here's what actually happens in the cold, and the handful of habits that keep winter flying safe.
