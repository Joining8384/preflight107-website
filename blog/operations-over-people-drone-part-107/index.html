Operations Over People: When You Can Fly Over Crowds Under Part 107

The default Part 107 rule is blunt: you can't fly over people who aren't part of your operation. But the 2021 \"Operations Over People\" rule opened the door — if your drone fits one of four categories. It's the difference between legally shooting a wedding, a concert, or a busy street and grounding yourself. Here's how the four categories work, which one your drone likely falls into, and where the hard line on crowds still sits.
