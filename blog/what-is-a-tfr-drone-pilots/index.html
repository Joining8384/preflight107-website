What Is a TFR? Temporary Flight Restrictions Explained for Drone Pilots

A TFR is the airspace restriction you can't see — there's no line on the sectional, no tower to call, nothing on the ground to warn you. The field that was perfectly legal last weekend can be a federal no-fly zone today, and you'd never know unless you checked. Here's what TFRs are, the common types that catch drone pilots, why "temporary" is exactly what makes them dangerous, and how to check for them before every single flight.
