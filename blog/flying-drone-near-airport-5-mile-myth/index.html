Flying a Drone Near an Airport: The 5-Mile Rule Is a Myth

\"You have to stay 5 miles from any airport\" is the most repeated piece of drone advice online — and it's been wrong for years. There is no blanket 5-mile rule, and you don't have to call the tower. What actually governs flying near an airport is the airspace class, not the distance, and it's simpler than the myth. Here's what the rule really is, where the old one came from, and how to check whether you're clear to fly in 30 seconds.
