The Drone Pre-Flight Checklist That Actually Prevents Crashes

Nearly every drone lost to something other than weather was lost to a step somebody skipped. A cracked prop nobody looked at, a home point set in the car, an RTH altitude lower than the tree line. Part 107 legally requires a preflight inspection — but the version that saves aircraft is more specific than the regulation. Here's a checklist split into the three moments that matter: before you leave, on site, and after you land.
