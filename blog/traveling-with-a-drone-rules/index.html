Traveling With a Drone: Flights, Batteries, and Foreign Rules

Taking your drone on a trip is mostly easy — until it isn't. Airlines have hard rules about lithium batteries, some countries will confiscate a drone at customs, and a national park will fine you for a launch that would be perfectly legal a mile outside the boundary. Here's how to pack it, what the battery rules actually are, and the homework to do before you fly your drone anywhere that isn't home.
