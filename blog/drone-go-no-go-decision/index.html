The Go/No-Go Call: How to Actually Decide Whether to Fly Today

Props on, batteries warm, and that quiet hesitation — should you fly or not? Most pilots make this call on gut feeling, which is exactly how good pilots end up launching on days they shouldn't. There's a better way, and it's the same structured process the FAA teaches every airman. Here's how to turn the go/no-go decision from a guess into a system: the PAVE checklist for drones, the personal-minimums trick, the five attitudes that talk you into bad launches, and how to make the call before you ever leave the truck.
