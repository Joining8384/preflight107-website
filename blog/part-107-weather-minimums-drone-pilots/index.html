Part 107 Weather Minimums: The Rules Most Drone Pilots Get Wrong

Wind gets all the attention, but it isn't the weather rule that gets pilots violated. Part 107 sets hard, legal weather minimums — 3 statute miles of visibility and specific distances from clouds — and a surprising number of certified pilots can't recite them from memory. Break one and you're operating illegally, even on a calm, sunny-looking day. Here's every Part 107 weather minimum in plain English, why each one exists, and how to actually confirm them before you launch.
