The FAA Deadlines That Expire Without Warning — and How to Never Miss One

Here's the uncomfortable part of staying legal as a drone pilot: the FAA does not send you a reminder. Your registration quietly expires after three years. Your Part 107 currency lapses at 24 months. And the day one of them lapses, your perfectly normal flight becomes an illegal one — same drone, same spot, same pilot. This is the maintenance side of being a drone pilot nobody talks about: the deadlines that expire on their own, why they're so easy to miss, and a simple system to make sure one never catches you off guard.
