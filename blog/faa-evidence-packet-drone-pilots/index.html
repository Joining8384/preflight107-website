The FAA Evidence Packet — The Post-Flight Document That Saves You When Someone Asks "Were You Sure?

Most pilots build neither — and end up reconstructing the story from memory months later, when an insurance adjuster or FAA inspector finally calls. Here's what an Evidence Packet contains, when you'll wish you had one, and how to build them automatically.
