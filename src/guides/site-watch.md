**Site Watch** keeps an eye on the forecast at your favorite launch spots so you don't have to. Tell it when you usually fly at a Saved Site, and PreFlight 107 sends you a notification when that window looks good to fly.

*(Site Watch is included on Free for 1 site. Pro and Pro+ can watch every Saved Site and get the extra alerts below.)*

## Turning on Site Watch

1. On the **Dashboard**, tap the **bookmark icon** next to *Change Location* to open **Saved Sites**. (You need to be signed in to save sites.)
2. Don't have a site yet? Tap **Save this location** and give it a name.
3. Tap the **bell** next to the site.
4. Turn on **Watch this site**, then choose:
   - **Days you fly here**: tap the days to turn them on or off.
   - **Flying window**: the *From* and *To* hours you usually fly.
   - **Wind limit**: taken from the drone selected on your Dashboard. If you switch drones later, tap **Use [drone]** to update it.
5. Tap **Save**. If PreFlight 107 asks to send notifications, tap **Allow**.

A watched site shows a cyan bell and a **Watching** label in your Saved Sites list.

## The alerts

| Alert | When it arrives | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🟢 **Good to fly** | The evening before your window (from 6 PM) when the forecast looks good. If it didn't look good that evening, we check again about 2 hours before your window. | Free, Pro, Pro+ |
| ⚠️ **Conditions changed** | If the forecast turns bad after we said it looked good, or turns good after it didn't. Checked every hour until your window starts. | Pro, Pro+ |
| 🚫 **New TFR nearby** | When a new TFR shows up within 5 nautical miles of the site. | Pro, Pro+ |

Tap any alert to open the **Dashboard on that site** with its live conditions.

## What "good to fly" means

A window counts as good when the forecast has **at least 2 hours in a row** (or the whole window, if it's only 1 hour) where:

- The **Fly Now Score** is 80 or higher
- Wind is **under your drone's limit**, and gusts aren't far above it
- Rain chance is **under 50%**, with no thunderstorms
- Visibility is **3 miles or better**

The alert tells you the best stretch of time plus the wind and gusts.

## No spam, promise

- **At most 1 forecast alert per site per day**
- **No alerts between 10 PM and 6 AM.** Anything that comes up overnight waits until morning.
- No repeats while the forecast stays the same
- Alerts arrive in the language you picked in the app (English or Spanish), and times use your phone's time zone

## Changing or stopping a watch

Open **Saved Sites**, tap the **bell**, and either adjust the days, hours, or wind limit and tap **Save**, or tap **Stop watching**. Deleting a Saved Site also removes its watch.

## Good to know

- Site Watch is a **heads-up, not a flight authorization.** Before you launch, always check the Dashboard, airspace, TFRs, and LAANC for the actual time and place you fly.
- On Free, you can watch 1 site at a time. To move it, stop watching the current site first, or upgrade to Pro to watch them all.
- Site Watch needs PreFlight 107 **version 1.1.1 or later**.
