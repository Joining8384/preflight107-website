A **Mission Briefing** is a professional flight document — weather, airspace, NOTAMs, hazards, and your pilot and aircraft details — packaged into a tamper-verifiable PDF. It's what separates a professional operator from "a person with a drone." *(Mission Briefings are a Pro+ feature.)*

## Generating a Mission Briefing

1. Open the **Mission Briefing** flow and set the **location** and **date/time** for the job.
2. The app auto-fetches the weather, airspace, NOTAMs, and aviation hazards for that place and time — no manual data entry.
3. Add any job details: mission name, crew, notes.
4. Generate. You get a multi-page PDF with a **SHA-256 tamper hash** printed on it, independently verifiable at **preflight107.com/verify** — so a client (or the FAA) can confirm it hasn't been altered.

## What the Client Bundle adds

The **Client Bundle** merges your Mission Briefing with your **flight report** (your logged flight, times, and conditions) into a **single client-ready PDF** — one document that proves the whole job was planned and flown properly.

To build one:

1. Go to your flight log in the **Hangar** for the job.
2. Choose **Build client bundle**.
3. The app merges the attached Mission Briefing into the combined report and produces one PDF you can share straight from your phone.

## White-label it (Pro+)

On Pro+, **white-label client mode** replaces PreFlight branding with **your business logo and details** on every page — so the deliverable looks like it came from your company, not ours.

## Why it matters

Handing a client this document alongside the photos is the moment you stop looking like a hobbyist. It's also your protection: if anything is ever questioned, you have a timestamped, tamper-evident record of exactly what you planned and flew.
