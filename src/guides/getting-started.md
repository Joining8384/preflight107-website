Welcome aboard. This guide gets you from a fresh install to your first go/no-go check in a few minutes.

## 1. Download and open the app

PreFlight 107 is free to download on the App Store. On first launch, allow **location access** — the app uses your GPS to pull hyper-local weather and check the airspace at your exact position. Without it, the dashboard can't tailor anything to where you're standing.

## 2. Add your drone in the Hangar

Tap the **Hangar** tab and add the drone you fly. This matters: your aircraft's wind, gust, and temperature limits drive the safety warnings and flight-time estimates across the whole app. We support 70+ models across DJI, Autel, Skydio, Parrot, HoverAir, and more — pick yours, or choose "Custom / Other" and enter your own limits.

## 3. Read your dashboard

The **Home** dashboard is your pre-flight picture at a glance. From the top:

- **Fly Now Score** — a single 0–100 go/no-go number. Tap it to see exactly why.
- **Weather cards** — wind, gusts, temperature, visibility, cloud ceiling, precipitation, and more, pulled for your exact location.
- **Density Altitude** and **Air Quality & Smoke** — how thin the air is and whether smoke could cut your visibility.
- **Satellite Health & Visible Satellites** — GPS reliability, so you know your lock is strong before you launch.
- **Airspace** — the class you're in and whether you need LAANC authorization.

## 4. Set your location

The app defaults to your current GPS. To check conditions somewhere else — a job site, a client's property — tap the location control and enter a spot, or save it as a **Saved Site** so you can switch to it with one tap later.

## Next steps

Once you're comfortable with the dashboard, the [Fly Now Score guide](/help/understanding-fly-now-score) explains how the score is calculated, and if you fly commercially, the [Mission Briefing guide](/help/create-mission-briefing-client-bundle) shows how to build a client-ready flight report.
