title: "Setting Up Recurring Briefings"
excerpt: "Schedule Mission Briefings to generate and email themselves automatically — daily, weekly, or monthly — so a recurring job's paperwork is done before you wake up."
---

If you fly the same site on a schedule — a construction progress shoot every Monday, a daily inspection route — **Recurring Briefings** generate the Mission Briefing automatically and email it to you (or your client) on a schedule, so the paperwork is waiting instead of being one more thing to do. *(Recurring Briefings are a Pro+ feature.)*

## Creating a schedule

1. Open **Recurring Briefings** and tap to create a new schedule.
2. Pick the **location** — usually a Saved Site.
3. Choose the **frequency**: daily, weekly, or monthly, and the **time** it should run.
4. Set the **recipient email** — yourself, or a client who wants a standing report.
5. Save. From then on, the briefing generates with fresh weather, airspace, and hazard data for that spot and lands in the inbox automatically.

## Managing your schedules

All your active schedules appear in one list. You can **edit** the frequency or recipient, **pause**, or **delete** a schedule anytime. Each one shows when it last ran so you can confirm it's firing.

## Tips

- **Use a Saved Site** as the location so the briefing always targets the exact spot.
- **Set a client as the recipient** for a standing deliverable — a weekly branded briefing in their inbox is the kind of thing that keeps a contract.
- The email is sent from PreFlight 107 on your behalf, with a reply-to set so any client replies reach *you*, not us.

## Troubleshooting

If a scheduled briefing didn't arrive, first check your **spam folder**, then confirm the schedule is still **active** and the recipient email is correct in the schedule list.
