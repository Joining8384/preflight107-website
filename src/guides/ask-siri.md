title: 'Ask Siri: "Can I Fly?"'

---

With PreFlight 107 on iPhone, you can check conditions **without opening the app**. Just ask Siri. It's handy when your hands are full of drone, controller, and batteries.

*(Siri works on every plan. It needs PreFlight 107 version 1.1.1 or later on iOS 16 or newer.)*

## Before your first ask

1. **Update** PreFlight 107 and **open it once**. That's how Siri learns your latest Dashboard check, your selected drone, and your Saved Sites.
2. To check Saved Sites by voice, make sure you're **signed in** and have at least one site saved.

No setup is needed in the Shortcuts app. The phrases work right away.

## What to say

**Can I fly where I am?**
- "Hey Siri, **can I fly with PreFlight 107**?"
- "Hey Siri, **check my Fly Now Score in PreFlight 107**"
- "Hey Siri, **is it good to fly in PreFlight 107**?"

**Check one of your Saved Sites**
- "Hey Siri, **check a site in PreFlight 107**." Siri asks *which site?* Then say its name, like "Lake Field."

If Siri has trouble with "PreFlight 107," you can also say just **"PreFlight."**

## What Siri tells you

Siri answers out loud and shows a small card with your **Fly Now Score**, the verdict (**Clear to fly**, **Use caution**, or **Do not fly**), and the wind and gusts.

- **If you checked the Dashboard in the last 20 minutes** at the same spot, Siri reads you that exact verdict, including airspace, your drone's limits, and every other check, along with how many minutes ago it was.
- **Otherwise**, Siri does a fresh **weather check** for where you are, using your drone's wind limits, and reminds you to **check airspace and TFRs in the app** before you fly.
- **Saved Site checks** are always a fresh weather check for that site.

If there's a reason not to fly (for example, wind over your drone's limit), Siri says so.

## English and Spanish

- Say the phrases in the **language your Siri is set to**. In Spanish: "Oye Siri, ¿puedo volar con PreFlight 107?" or "Revisa un sitio en PreFlight 107."
- Siri's answer follows the **language you chose in PreFlight 107** (Settings → Preferences → Language).

## More ways to run it

Both actions also show up in the **Shortcuts** app and **Spotlight** under PreFlight 107. From Shortcuts, you can add "Can I Fly?" to your Home Screen, or assign it to the **Action button** on iPhones that have one.

## Troubleshooting

- **Siri doesn't recognize the phrase:** open PreFlight 107 once, then try again. Also check **Settings → Apps → PreFlight 107 → Siri** and make sure Siri is allowed for the app.
- **"Open PreFlight 107 once so I know where you are":** open the app so it can get a location fix, then ask again.
- **"You don't have any Saved Sites yet":** save a site from the Dashboard first (you need to be signed in).

Siri gives you a quick read. It doesn't replace your preflight check. Always confirm airspace, TFRs, and LAANC in the app before you launch.
