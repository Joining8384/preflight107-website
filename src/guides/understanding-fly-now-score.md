The **Fly Now Score** is the fastest read on your dashboard — one number from 0 to 100 that answers "should I fly right now?" so you don't have to mentally juggle five weather values.

## What the number means

- **Green (high score)** — conditions are well within safe limits. Good to go.
- **Amber (mid score)** — flyable, but something is marginal. Fly with awareness.
- **Red (low score)** — one or more conditions are outside safe limits. Reconsider.

The score is tuned to *your* aircraft, using the limits from the drone you selected in the Hangar — so the same weather can score differently for a light sub-250g drone versus a heavier one.

## What goes into it

The score blends the factors that actually decide a safe flight:

- **Wind and gusts** against your drone's rated limits
- **Visibility** and **cloud ceiling**
- **Flight category** (the overall weather picture)
- **Hazards and airspace** flags

## Tap it to see why

The most useful part: **tap the score** and it breaks down each factor, showing which ones are helping and which are dragging it down. Instead of a mystery number, you see exactly *why* — "gusts near your limit," "low ceiling," "airspace clear." That's what turns the score from a guess into a decision you can explain.

## Hard overrides

Some conditions are non-negotiable. If a factor crosses a hard safety limit — like gusts well beyond your aircraft's rating — the score reflects that as a clear no-go rather than averaging it away. The score won't tell you it's fine when a critical factor says it isn't.

## Remember: it's a tool, not the pilot

The Fly Now Score puts the right information in front of you, but the go/no-go decision is always yours as pilot in command. Use it as your first read, then apply your own judgment and personal minimums.
