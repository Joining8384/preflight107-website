## The three tiers

- **Free** — the safety essentials: Fly Now Score, Density Altitude, Air Quality, live ADS-B radar, LAANC grid ceilings, current weather, basic METAR, 3 flight logs, 1 drone, and 1 sample Mission Briefing. Aviation safety is never paywalled.
- **Pro** — the working pilot's toolkit: 15-day forecast, decoded aviation hazards, TFR alerts, Auto-Evidence PDF, unlimited flight logs and Hangar, Flying Insights, Client Bundle, 3D Wind Tower, Apple Wallet pilot card, and more.
- **Pro+** — built for client work: unlimited Mission Briefings, white-label client mode, recurring briefings, the Live Observer Link, AI Morning Brief, and unlimited AI Risk Assessments.

## Starting a subscription

Open the **paywall** (the Pro tab, or any locked feature) and choose **Pro** or **Pro+**, monthly or annual. New subscribers get a **7-day free Pro+ trial** — full access for a week, cancel anytime before it ends and you're not charged.

Billing is handled by **Apple** through your Apple ID. Your subscription unlocks features across all your devices signed into the same Apple ID.

## Restoring purchases

Switched phones, or features showing locked after you've paid? Open the paywall and tap **Restore Purchases**. This re-checks your Apple ID entitlements and unlocks everything you're entitled to. If it doesn't resolve immediately, fully close and reopen the app — the app re-verifies your tier on launch.

## Managing or canceling

Subscriptions are managed in your **Apple ID settings**, not inside the app (Apple's rules):

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone
2. Tap your name → **Subscriptions**
3. Select **PreFlight 107** to change tier, switch billing, or cancel

Canceling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the current period ends.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account, go to **Settings** in the app and choose account deletion. Your account is scheduled for permanent deletion after a 30-day grace period (in case you change your mind), and your data is purged after that. Note that **deleting your account is separate from canceling your subscription** — cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings too, or it keeps billing.
