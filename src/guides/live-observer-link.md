The **Live Observer Link** turns your Share Launch into a live, updating web page. Send it to a client, a crew member, or a spouse, and they can watch your conditions and status update in real time while you fly — no app install, just a link. *(The live option is a Pro+ feature; a one-time location snapshot is available on Pro.)*

## What the observer sees

The link opens a web page showing:

- Your **launch location** and conditions (wind, weather, flight category)
- **Airspace** and any **TFRs** at your spot
- A live **"updated Ns ago"** heartbeat so they know the session is active
- A **LIVE** badge while you're flying, or **Flight Ended** once you're done

One important note: the position is from **your phone's GPS**, not the drone itself — so it shows where *you* are set up, with conditions updating live. It's a situational-awareness and safety link, not a drone-tracking map.

## Starting a live link

1. On the **Map**, open **Share Launch**.
2. Toggle **Live Observer** on (Pro+), or leave it off for a one-time snapshot (Pro).
3. Choose **how long** it should stay live — 30 minutes up to several hours.
4. Start the link and share the URL however you like (text, email).

## It stops itself

For battery and privacy, the live link **auto-ends when your chosen time is up**, even if you forget to tap End Flight. You can also end it manually anytime. Once it ends, the link stops updating and shows "Flight Ended."

## Good uses

- A client who wants to know you're on site and flying
- A crew member or visual observer coordinating from elsewhere
- Peace of mind for family during a solo flight in a remote spot
