PreFlight107 Prep Support

PreFlight107 Prep helps you pass the FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot knowledge test and understand the rules behind it.

Contact us: support@preflight107.com
Tell us your device, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings), and what happened.

Restore my purchase

Sign in with the same account you used to buy (Settings → Account). Your purchases follow your PreFlight107 Prep account to any iPhone you sign in on. If Pro still doesn’t show, open the Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases”, which checks the purchases made with your Apple ID.

I want a refund

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

My sign-in code didn’t arrive

Check your spam or junk folder for an email from “PreFlight107 Prep”. Codes expire after 10 minutes. Make sure you typed your own email address, then request a new code.

How do AI tutor messages work?

Each message you send uses one from your balance, which shows in the tutor and in Settings. New accounts get 3 free messages; Pro includes 500, Pro Plus 2,000, and a Tutor Pack adds 500. If the tutor can’t answer because of a problem on our side, the message isn’t counted.

I think a question or answer is wrong

Tap “Report a problem” under the question or the tutor’s answer. Every report is checked against the FAA source, and fixes ship in app updates.

Move my progress to a new phone

Sign in on both phones with the same account. Your answers, practice exams, lessons, and study settings sync automatically.

Delete my account and data

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and synced data from our servers. To clear progress without deleting your account, use Settings → Reset all progress. See the Privacy Policy for details.
