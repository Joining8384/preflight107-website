PreFlight107 Prep Privacy Policy

Mcuztoms LLC · Effective October 18, 2026

This policy explains what PreFlight107 Prep, the iPhone app and its web version on preflight107.com (together, “the app”), collects, why, who it’s shared with, and the choices you have. The app is published by Mcuztoms LLC (“we”, “us”).

The short version
  • You can study without an account. Your progress then stays on your device: your phone, or your browser in the web version.
  • We don’t show ads, sell your data, use cookies, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • An account is needed only for purchases, the AI tutor, and syncing between devices. You can delete it in the app at any time.
  • AI tutor messages go to a third-party AI service to create answers, only after you agree. We don’t store what you write to the tutor.

1. Information that stays on your device

The app saves your study progress on your device: the questions you’ve answered, practice exams, lessons you’ve finished, points and streaks, the practice session or exam you have in progress, your study settings (whether you’re studying or already certified, your test date, and reminder settings), and any test result you enter. Study reminders are scheduled on your phone; no reminder information is sent to us. If you don’t sign in, this information isn’t sent anywhere, except as described for problem reports and the AI tutor below.

In the web version, the same information, and your sign-in if you sign in, is kept in your browser’s storage on your computer (local storage and IndexedDB). That storage is needed for the web version to work, and nothing else; we don’t use cookies.

2. Your account (optional)

You can create an account with Sign in with Apple or with your email address and a one-time code. When you do, we store:

  • An account identifier, and the user identifier Apple provides if you use Sign in with Apple.
  • Your email address. With Sign in with Apple, you can choose to share a private relay address instead of your real one.
  • Your name, if you choose to share it through Sign in with Apple.
  • Your study progress and study settings, so they sync across your devices, including any test result you enter (pass or fail, your score, and the ACS codes from your test report), and the practice session or exam you have in progress, so you can pick it up on another device.
  • Your AI tutor message balance: the messages included with purchases or given free, and how many you’ve used. This doesn’t include what you wrote.

We use this information only to sign you in, sync your progress, provide what you’ve purchased, and keep track of your tutor messages. If you sign in with email, we use your address to send your sign-in codes. We don’t send marketing email from the app.

3. The AI tutor

Before your first tutor message, the app asks for your permission. When you send a message, the app sends it to our server along with your recent messages in that conversation and, if you’re asking about a practice question, that question and the answer you picked. Our server sends this to a third-party AI service provider to create and fact-check the answer.

  • We don’t store the content of your tutor messages. Our server records that a message was used (for your balance) and what it cost to answer. Our server’s operational logs, which are kept for a short time, can include notes from the automatic fact-check for troubleshooting.
  • The AI service provider processes your messages only to provide the service. Under its commercial terms, it may not use them to train its AI models. It may keep them for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.
  • Please don’t include personal information, such as your name, address, or phone number, in tutor messages.

4. Problem reports

If you tap “Report a problem” on a question or a tutor answer, we receive: the question’s ID, the reason you chose, any comment you write, the answer you picked and the answer key, the app version, and a random ID created for this installation of the app. That random ID isn’t linked to your account or your identity. For generated weather practice, the report includes the practice report; for a tutor answer, it includes your question and the tutor’s answer. We use reports only to review and fix the app’s content, and we receive an email copy of each one.

5. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and unlock what you bought. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and your purchase history. In the web version, which sells nothing, our server asks RevenueCat whether your account has Pro, using your account identifier. We never receive your payment details.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only with these service providers, only as needed to run the app. They are required to protect it as this policy describes:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in, and server functions (servers in the United States).
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation and access to paid features.
  • Resend: sends sign-in code emails, and our internal alerts such as problem reports.
  • A third-party AI service provider: creates and fact-checks AI tutor answers.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

7. What we don’t do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of your personal information for advertising.
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites, and no analytics or advertising tools in the app.
  • No cookies, and no third-party content in the web version: its code, fonts, and images come from our own website.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

8. How long we keep information, and how to delete it

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, synced progress, study settings, and tutor balance from our servers.
  • Reset your progress in the app: Settings → Reset all progress. This clears it on your device and, if you’re signed in, on our servers.
  • In the web version, you can also remove everything it keeps in your browser by clearing this website’s data in your browser’s settings.
  • If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also stop using it for this app in your Apple ID settings.
  • Records of purchases are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed for receipts, refunds, and legal obligations.
  • Problem reports are kept as long as they help us improve the content. They aren’t linked to your account.
  • Otherwise, account information is kept until you delete your account.

9. Children

The app isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10. Security

Information is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers. On our servers, access controls limit each account to its own data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to how it’s used. You can delete your account in the app at any time, or email us to make a request. We don’t sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we’ll tell you in the app.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: support@preflight107.com
