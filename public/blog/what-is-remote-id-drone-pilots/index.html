What Is Remote ID? The Drone "Digital License Plate" Explained

Since March 2024, the FAA has been enforcing Remote ID — the rule that makes most drones broadcast their location and identity the whole time they're in the air. It's the single biggest compliance change for everyday pilots since registration, and a lot of people still aren't sure whether it applies to them, what their drone is actually transmitting, or who can see it. Here's what Remote ID is, the three legal ways to comply, exactly who's exempt, and what your drone broadcasts every time you fly.
