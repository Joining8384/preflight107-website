Controlled vs. Uncontrolled Airspace: A Drone Pilot's Guide to the Airspace Classes

Every spot you fly is inside one of two kinds of airspace — controlled or uncontrolled — and which one you're standing in decides whether you can just launch or whether you need FAA authorization first. The whole system comes down to airports and the letters A through G. Here's what controlled vs. uncontrolled actually means, a plain-English tour of each airspace class, the Class E trap that catches careful pilots, and how to know which one is over your head before you fly.
