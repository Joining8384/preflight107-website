Flying Near Thunderstorms: How Far Is Safe, and How to Know

A thunderstorm on the horizon looks like a problem for later — plenty of blue sky between you and it. That gap is exactly what fools pilots. Thunderstorms throw hazards miles beyond the dark clouds: gust fronts that arrive before the rain, and lightning that strikes from what looks like clear air. Here's how far you should actually stay from a storm, the two rules that keep you safe, and how to check storm risk before you ever unfold the props.
