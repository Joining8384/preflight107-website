Can You Fly a Drone at Night? The Part 107 Night Rules, Explained

For years, flying a drone after dark meant applying for a special FAA waiver and waiting weeks. That changed in 2021 — night flying is now legal by the rules, no waiver required, if you meet two conditions. Here's exactly what those two conditions are, what counts as "anti-collision lighting," how the rules differ for recreational flyers, what "night" and "civil twilight" actually mean, and the one thing that gets harder after dark that no rule change can fix.
